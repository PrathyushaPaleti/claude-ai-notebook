# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Runner

Here is a one-day meal plan for a vegan athlete preparing for a marathon race:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (120g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Gluten-free oatmeal (80g dry) with coconut milk (240ml), banana (120g), and chia seeds (15g). Fresh berries (100g).
    * Protein: 12g, Fat: 14g, Carbs: 75g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Rice cakes (50g) with tahini (25g) and dates (40g).
    * Protein: 8g, Fat: 10g, Carbs: 48g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Lentil soup (300ml) made with red lentils (100g dry), vegetable broth, carrots (80g), celery (60g), and spinach (50g). Gluten-free bread (70g) with avocado (60g).
    * Protein: 28g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 68g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Smoothie with plant-based protein powder (30g), almond-free seed butter (20g), mango (150g), and oat milk (300ml).
    * Protein: 25g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 48g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Baked tofu (180g) with quinoa (80g cooked), roasted sweet potato (200g), and steamed broccoli (200g) with olive oil (15ml).
    * Protein: 32g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 85g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Pumpkin seeds (30g) with dried apricots (50g).
    * Protein: 15g, Fat: 13g, Carbs: 48g
